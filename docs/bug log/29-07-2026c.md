# 29-07-2026c — `> /config` replayed inside the conversation body

## Symptom

Open `/config`, press `q`. The wipe is followed by the replayed transcript under
its header:

```
Conversation history (4 messages, still sent to the model):

> Say the magic word
…
> /config
```

`/config` is never sent to the model. Reading it back under "still sent to the
model" states something false about what the model has seen.

## Cause

`session-modes.ts` calls `recordTranscriptPrompt(submitted)` from the Enter
handler — before `dispatchCommand` has decided whether the input is a command or
a prompt. Every submission landed in the record, slash commands included.

`transcript-record.ts` is explicit that the record holds the conversation only;
menu chrome, approval prompts and the rest of the ephemeral UI are deliberately
left out. A slash command's *output* was already excluded. Its echo was not.

## Fix

`slash-commands.ts` now exports `isSlashCommand(input)`, and the record call is
gated on it.

The predicate is shared with `command-dispatcher.ts`'s own fallthrough rather
than duplicated, so the record and the dispatcher cannot disagree about what
counts as a command. That matters for the *unknown* case: `/confgi` is rejected
with "No command: /confgi" and never reaches the model either, so gating on a
list of known commands would have recorded a turn that was never sent.

Hand-typed tool invocations (`read(path="x")`) stay recorded — they do not reach
the model either, but their call line and result preview are written by the
transcript renderer and so are already in the record; dropping only the echo
would replay a headless result.

## Coverage

- `tests/cli/session-modes.test.ts` — the Enter handler records a normal prompt
  and records nothing for `/config`, `  /config`, or an unknown `/confgi`.
- `tests/e2e/tty-config-keeps-transcript.e2e.json` — `screenAbsent: ["> /config"]`
  after both `/config` round trips, alongside the existing block assertion that
  the real turns do come back.
