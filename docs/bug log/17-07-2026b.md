# Bug Log

## Tool-approval controls line persisted in the transcript after confirm/deny

**Symptom:** After confirming or denying a tool call, the `Enter to confirm · Esc
to deny` controls line stayed behind in the transcript instead of vanishing once
the decision was made.

**Cause (`src/cli/tool-approval.ts`):** the raw-key handler in
`readToolApprovalMenu` wrote a bare `\n` on Enter/Escape. In the footer case the
hint is drawn *absolute* on the scroll region's **bottom margin**
(`drawToolApprovalHintAbsolute`), so that newline scrolled the hint text up one
row into the transcript — and the `finally` block's clear then landed on the
now-blank bottom row, missing it. In the inline case (`!isFooterUIActive()`) the
newline simply left the hint sitting on its own line with nothing erasing it.

**Fix:**
- Dropped the `\n` from both the Enter and Escape branches of `onKey` — nothing is
  scrolled, so the hint stays put on its row.
- `confirmToolCallInteractive`'s `finally` now erases it for both layouts: footer
  runs clear the exact absolute row (`\x1b[row;1H\x1b[2K`), inline TTY runs clear
  the parked cursor's line in place (`\r\x1b[2K`).

**Coverage:** `tests/cli/tool-approval.test.ts` — inline "erases the hint line in
place on confirm" and footer "writes no bare newline on confirm"; the two
`tty-tool-approval-preview*` scenarios now assert `screenAbsent` for the controls
line on the approve step (verified failing when the `\n` is reintroduced).
